# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# CartWise

> **Shop Smarter. Spend Less.**

---

# 1. Overview

CartWise is a full-stack web application that helps users compare grocery prices across multiple quick-commerce platforms such as Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Amazon Fresh, BB Now, Flipkart Minutes, and more.

Instead of manually checking each platform, users can search once and instantly compare prices to make the smartest purchasing decision.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Online grocery shopping has become extremely convenient, but comparing prices across multiple platforms is still a manual and time-consuming process.

A user who wants to purchase groceries currently has to:

- Open Blinkit
- Search the product
- Remember the price
- Open Zepto
- Search again
- Open Instamart
- Compare prices manually

This process wastes both time and money.

---

# 3. Our Solution

CartWise provides a single platform where users can search for any grocery item and compare prices across multiple quick-commerce platforms in one place.

Instead of visiting several applications, users receive all available prices on a single screen.

---

# 4. Vision

To become the smartest shopping companion that helps users make better purchasing decisions by comparing prices, delivery charges, offers, coupons, and complete shopping carts across multiple platforms.

---

# 5. Target Audience

### Primary Users

- Students
- Working Professionals
- Families
- Frequent Online Grocery Buyers

### Future Users

- Small Businesses
- Restaurants
- Hostels
- PG Residents

---

# 6. Objectives

The primary objectives of CartWise are:

- Reduce shopping costs
- Save users' time
- Improve price transparency
- Simplify grocery comparison
- Help users make informed purchasing decisions

---

# 7. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

Version 1 will include:

- Product Search
- Price Comparison
- Product Availability
- Cheapest Platform Highlight
- Responsive User Interface

---

# 8. Future Scope

Future versions may include:

- User Login
- Wishlist
- Shopping Cart Comparison
- Coupon Comparison
- Delivery Charge Comparison
- Price History
- Notifications
- AI Shopping Suggestions
- Personalized Recommendations
- Analytics Dashboard

---

# 9. Success Metrics

CartWise will be considered successful if users can:

- Find products quickly
- Compare prices within seconds
- Reduce shopping expenses
- Spend less time comparing platforms

---

# 10. Out of Scope

The first release will NOT include:

- Payments
- Order Placement
- Inventory Management
- Admin Dashboard
- Reviews
- Seller Portal

---

# 11. Conclusion

CartWise aims to simplify online grocery shopping by providing users with one place to compare prices across multiple platforms.

The goal is to save both time and money while delivering a simple, fast, and intuitive shopping experience.
